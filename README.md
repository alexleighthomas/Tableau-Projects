<h2> 🌍 Gapminder Health Dashboard </h2>
<p1> 

<img width="928" height="399" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d8f85f8e-6533-4e6a-9fa0-6e658b1cb6d6" />


The Global Health Insights Dashboard explores:

- Life expectancy differences across continents

- Trends over time for high‑performing countries

- Gender‑based population distribution

- Relationships between BMI and life expectancy

- Additional health metrics such as blood pressure, cholesterol, and cancer incidence </p1>

<h4> Key Insight: </h4>
<p1> Higher‑income regions show significantly higher life expectancy. For example, Europe averaged 77.42 years, while Africa averaged 58.73 years, a difference of 18.69 years. Cardiovascular risk factors also varied by region, aligning with known dietary and lifestyle patterns. </p1>
